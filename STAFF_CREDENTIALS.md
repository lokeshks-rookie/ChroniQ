## 3. Quick Role Summary & Login URL

- **Login URL**: [http://localhost:5173/login](http://localhost:5173/login)
- **Role Scopes**:
  - `super_admin`: Full system oversight, access to `/admin/hospitals` and all hospitals' data.
  - `hospital_admin`: Managing doctor schedules, departments, walk-ins, and hospital-scoped staff.
  - `receptionist`: Managing check-in desks, walk-in token issuance, queue movements, and patient intake.
  - `doctor`: Access to `/doctor`, live consultation queue, availability toggles, and consultation records.
